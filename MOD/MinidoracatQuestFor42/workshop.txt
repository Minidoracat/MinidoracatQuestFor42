description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Click the trophy icon on the family toolbar to open Quests & Achievements
description=[*] Check today's quests on the Daily quests page; they advance as you play
description=[*] Complete quests and unlock achievements to earn points, then see your rank on the Leaderboards page
description=[*] On servers with the Economy mod, exchange points for Survivor Coins on the Exchange page
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Daily and weekly quests[/b]: drawn from the themes you prefer, with one swap a day
description=[*] [b]Server-wide quest and server goal[/b]: one daily quest everyone shares, and a weekly goal the whole server works on together
description=[*] [b]Streaks and newcomer quests[/b]: milestone rewards for playing day after day, and a guided start for new players
description=[*] [b]Timed events and hordes[/b]: the host schedules periods that multiply quest points, or sends hordes for everyone to clear for points
description=[*] [b]Achievements and titles[/b]: five tiers per achievement, a title at Platinum, and server-wide announcements for high tiers
description=[*] [b]Leaderboards and seasons[/b]: 8 leaderboard categories, with rewards and titles by rank when a season ends
description=[*] [b]Point exchange[/b]: with the Economy mod, turn points into Survivor Coins
description=[*] [b]Admin panel[/b]: thresholds, quests, seasons and exchange are all tuned in game, and every change can be undone
description=[/list]
